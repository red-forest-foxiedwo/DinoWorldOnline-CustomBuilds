General Information:

  1. Creator: RED FOREST
  2. Password: There is no protection.

Cheat Details (for Gamers):

  1. Brief: No level cap
  2. Details: Modders can give you immensely high XP or even negative XP. If your XP (integer) crosses the range [0, 2 million] then your level becomes 0. For some cases, the upper bound is not 2m but 20m. To prevent that, while also keep every other experience else same as the official app, this mod removes those boundaries. Please note that this mod does not fully remove a hacker's ability to make you level 0. Instead it makes you harder to be turned into level 0. Also if you are in the negative XP you are in the range that allows nonzero level, you become level 1 until your xp is 100.. But you would still require huge Positive XP to get back to greater than XP 0, which requires a Huge-XP-giving mod. Even above 20 million, you remain at level 10.
